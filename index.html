The problem

Many students and first-time investors in Saudi Arabia want to start investing but find market terms confusing. Someone who hears about a dividend or an index such as TASI has no simple place to learn what these mean, and may end up following social media tips instead.

Our solution

Tadawul Academy is a small, structured website. It explains the basics step by step, defines key terms, lists well-known listed companies, and offers a calculator that shows how regular saving could add up over time.

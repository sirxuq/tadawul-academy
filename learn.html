Market basics

The Saudi Exchange, known as Tadawul, is where shares of Saudi companies are bought and sold. A share is a small piece of ownership in a company. When the company does well, its share price may rise and it may pay dividends. When it does badly, the price may fall.

The Capital Market Authority (CMA) regulates the market. Companies are listed on the Main Market or on Nomu, the parallel market for smaller companies with lighter listing rules.

Glossary

TermMeaning
ShareA unit of ownership in a company.
DividendA part of company profit paid to shareholders.
Index (TASI)A number that tracks how a group of listed companies performs overall.
IPOThe first time a company offers its shares to the public.
SectorA group of similar businesses, such as banks or telecom.
DiversificationSpreading money across different investments to reduce risk.

First steps

  1. Learn the terms above before investing.
  2. Choose a broker licensed by the CMA.
  3. Start with a small amount you can afford to lose.
  4. Spread your money across sectors.
  5. Review your choices regularly and keep learning.
